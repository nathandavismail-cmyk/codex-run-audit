# Community conduct

Treat people respectfully. Critique code and evidence rather than personal characteristics. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, and disclosure of private material are not acceptable.

Keep issue discussions relevant and constructive. Do not post secrets or proprietary transcripts as reproductions. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, lock discussions, or restrict participation, explaining decisions where appropriate.

For harassment, threats, privacy exposure or other platform abuse, use GitHub’s
[Report content / Report abuse process](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
That channel reaches GitHub Support; it is not a private project-maintainer inbox.
Do not publish sensitive allegations or personal information in an issue.
Non-sensitive moderation requests may be raised with repository maintainers.
Security vulnerabilities follow SECURITY.md rather than conduct or bug reports.
