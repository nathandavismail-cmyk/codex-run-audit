# Local diagnosis exercise

These cases are invented. Do not provide credentials or private session history.
A facilitator will assign cases and either a raw export or a rendered report.

For each case, explain:

1. Did the agent turn finish? What is the evidence?
2. What is known about input usage and failed or repeated commands?
3. Can you establish that the developer's task succeeded?
4. What would you check next? What remains unknown?

Record your elapsed time, any help needed, and what was confusing. “Unknown” is
an acceptable answer when the available evidence does not establish a fact.
You may stop at any time. Do not inspect facilitator answers during the exercise.
